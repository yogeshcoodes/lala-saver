Security & Architecture Notice

Lala Saver is an independent Graphical User Interface (GUI). It acts strictly as a visual wrapper for public command-line tools. Because it is an indie software without a paid corporate publisher certificate, Windows Defender or your antivirus may flag the executable as "unrecognized" or suspicious. This is a standard false positive. To proceed safely, simply click "More info" followed by "Run anyway".
